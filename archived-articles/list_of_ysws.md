# List of You Ship We Ships
A "you ship, we ship" is a program where a participant first builds and "ships" a project, and in return, they receive a reward, often hardware, from the organizer.

- Blueprint: Design a hardware project, up to $400 to build it! (ends 2025-12-01)
- Shiba Arcade: Make a game with Godot, earn SSS, and get invited to build an arcade in Tokyo (flight stipends available!) (ends 2025-10-20)
- Siege: Code every week, get prizes. Make it to the end, get a framework! (ends 2025-11-30)
- Emerge: create an emergent behaviour, place pixels on the group canvas, get the canvas shipped to you! (ends 2025-11-01)
- Reactive: Ship a website built with React, get $15 to buy a domain. (ends 2025-10-16)
- Fanpage: Ship a project that celebrates a fandom, and we ship you something related to it! (ends 2025-10-17)
- Hackberry: Built a machine learning project in python, get a personalized raspberry pi kit! (ends 2025-10-13)
- Waveform: Make your own waveform visualizer, get a grant to buy music (ends 2025-09-26)
- birthday-cards: You ship a birthday themed project we ship a birthday card! (ends 2026-08-12)
- Miniverge: Spend 2 hours on a bot, get stickers and a chance for a Blåhaj or Minecraft! (ends 2025-09-28)
- Penumbra: Make shaders, and we'll ship you GPUs and posters! (ends 2025-10-08)
- Playphone: Work together to built a godot game, get a custom hackclub badge (ends 2025-12-01)
- Clutter: You ship something to organize your digital life. We ship you a grant to buy something to organize your physical life. (ends 2025-09-17)
- Fusering: Design a keyring, get your keyring and a carabiner to hang it from! (ends 2025-09-30)
- iFrame: Draw or edit images using any programming language and we will hang your custom image on HQ wall and send a copy to you
- BakeBuild: Design a cookie cutter, get it shipped!
- HackCraft: Create a Minecraft mod, get Stickers, Minecraft or a server and terraria!
- Summer of Making: Build stuff. Get stuff. Repeat. (ends 2025-09-30)
- Sprig: Build a JS game and play it on your own console.
- Boba Drops: Build a website and get boba!
